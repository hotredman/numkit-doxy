5// Additional filter-form conversions: sos ↔ tf, sos ↔ zpk, tf ↔ ss,
6// sos ↔ ss, zpk ↔ ss, plus tf2zpk (gain-explicit alias of tf2zp).
7//
8// State-space form is the 4-tuple (A, B, C, D):
9// A : N×N state-transition matrix
10// B : N×1 input vector
11// C : 1×N output vector
12// D : 1×1 direct feed-through scalar
13// Single-input single-output controllable canonical form is used.
