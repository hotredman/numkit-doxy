double x0,
std::pmr::memory_resource * mr = nullptr )

Scalar root-finder, initial-guess form (x = fzero(fn, x0)).

Expands an outward bracket around x0 until a sign change is detected, then runs Brent's method.

The callback receives a 1-element args (the scalar evaluation point) and writes its scalar result into outs[0].

Parameters
fnCallback (scalar in, scalar out).
x0Initial guess.
mrMemory resource (nullptr → process default).

Scalar root.
